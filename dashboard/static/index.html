01

Why does the map show red/orange areas near me when it is only drizzling today?

The map shows two metrics: Active Risk (today's rain) and Terrain Vulnerability (natural soil and slope). Even if today is light rain, red/orange zones highlight steep hills or low-lying areas that could become dangerous during heavy downpours.

02

How can I check if my area will face flood or landslide risk tomorrow or in 48 hours?

Select your location in the search bar, then click the forecast dropdown (24 Hours, 48 Hours, or 72 Hours). The dashboard uses IMD rainfall forecasts to calculate future Active Risk scores and update the map overlay.

03

What does it mean if a nearby dam shows a 'RED Alert' badge?

A RED Alert means the dam's water level has reached its maximum threshold, and excess water may be released through spillways. If you live downstream or near riverbanks, prepare to move to safer locations as advised by local authorities.

04

Who should I contact immediately if floodwaters start rising near my house?

Use the Emergency Contacts section on the dashboard to quickly call local emergency services: Dist. EOC / IMD (1077), Fire & Rescue (101), Police (100), Ambulance (108), or NDRF (011-24363260).

05

Can I search for any town or village in Kerala on this dashboard?

Yes, you can type any location within Kerala (e.g., Munnar, Wayanad, Chalakudy, Valavoor). If you search outside Kerala, the system will remind you to search within Kerala boundaries for accurate predictions.
